## Probate in Waukegan

Waukegan is the Lake County seat, and all Lake County estates are filed here, at the **Lake County Courthouse, 18 N County St, Waukegan, IL 60085**. Lake County is the 19th Judicial Circuit. Filing is electronic, but the original will is filed in person.

That applies wherever in Lake County the person lived: Gurnee, Libertyville, Lake Forest, Highland Park and the rest all file in Waukegan. The [Lake County probate guide](/blog/lake-county-il-probate) walks through the local process.

## Property in more than one county or state

If the person owned real estate outside Lake County, the Illinois estate is still opened in the county where they lived. Real estate in another state can need a second court case there. Raise it at the first meeting.

## How long it takes

A straightforward Illinois estate usually takes about 9 to 12 months from opening to closing. It cannot finish much sooner, because creditors get at least six months from the first published notice to file claims. Contested or complex estates can take 12 to 24 months or longer. See the [Illinois probate timeline by county](/resources/illinois-probate-timeline-by-county).

## Is probate needed at all?

Not always. Illinois lets heirs use a small estate affidavit instead of probate when the estate has no real estate and its personal property totals $150,000 or less (for deaths on or after August 15, 2025; $100,000 for earlier deaths). A house in the deceased person's name alone does need probate before it can be sold. [How probate works in Illinois](/resources/how-probate-works-illinois) explains the steps.
