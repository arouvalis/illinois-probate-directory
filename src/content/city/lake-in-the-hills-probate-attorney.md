## Probate for a Lake in the Hills estate

Lake in the Hills is in McHenry County. Estates are filed at the **McHenry County Courthouse, 2200 N Seminary Ave, Woodstock, IL 60098**. McHenry County is the 22nd Judicial Circuit. Filing is electronic, but the original will is filed in person at the Clerk's office.

McHenry County has a lighter docket than Cook County, so court dates usually come sooner.

## Living trusts

Many people looking for a probate attorney here are also asking about living trusts. The two are closely related:

- A house or account held in a **living trust** passes to the people named in the trust without a probate case. The successor trustee handles it.
- A trust only covers what was actually transferred into it. Assets left in the person's own name may still need probate.
- A trust is private. A probate file is a public court record.

Most offices listed on this page handle both probate and estate planning. Each profile shows the practice areas the listing reports.

## How long it takes

A straightforward Illinois estate usually takes about 9 to 12 months from opening to closing. It cannot finish much sooner, because creditors get at least six months from the first published notice to file claims. Contested or complex estates can take 12 to 24 months or longer. See the [Illinois probate timeline by county](/resources/illinois-probate-timeline-by-county).

## Is probate needed at all?

Not always. Illinois lets heirs use a small estate affidavit instead of probate when the estate has no real estate and its personal property totals $150,000 or less (for deaths on or after August 15, 2025; $100,000 for earlier deaths). A house in the deceased person's name alone does need probate before it can be sold. [How probate works in Illinois](/resources/how-probate-works-illinois) explains the steps.
