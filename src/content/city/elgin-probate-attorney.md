## Probate for an Elgin estate

Most of Elgin is in Kane County. Kane County estates are filed at the **Kane County Courthouse, 100 S 3rd St, Geneva, IL 60134** (Probate Administration, Room 112). The Kane County Judicial Center on Route 38 in St. Charles is a different building and does not handle probate.

The eastern part of Elgin is in Cook County. If the person lived on that side, the estate is filed with the Probate Division at the Richard J. Daley Center in Chicago. The property tax bill shows the county.

## Choosing an Elgin probate attorney

Illinois does not require an attorney for every estate, but most executors use one when there is a house, a dispute or a creditor problem. When you call, ask whether the attorney files regularly in the county that will handle the estate, how the fee is charged (hourly or flat), and who in the office will answer your questions.

## How long it takes

A straightforward Illinois estate usually takes about 9 to 12 months from opening to closing. It cannot finish much sooner, because creditors get at least six months from the first published notice to file claims. Contested or complex estates can take 12 to 24 months or longer. See the [Illinois probate timeline by county](/resources/illinois-probate-timeline-by-county).

## Is probate needed at all?

Not always. Illinois lets heirs use a small estate affidavit instead of probate when the estate has no real estate and its personal property totals $150,000 or less (for deaths on or after August 15, 2025; $100,000 for earlier deaths). A house in the deceased person's name alone does need probate before it can be sold. [How probate works in Illinois](/resources/how-probate-works-illinois) explains the steps.
