# What Does an Executor Do in Illinois?

Being named executor can feel like a second job that arrives at the worst possible time. This guide describes the role in general terms so you know what questions to bring to a probate attorney.

## The role in plain English

The executor is the person who looks after the estate until it is settled. What that involves depends on the estate, and an Illinois probate attorney can tell you exactly what applies to yours.

## Why it cannot be rushed

In an Illinois probate estate, creditors get at least six months from the first published notice to file claims, and the estate cannot close before that period ends. A straightforward estate usually takes about 9 to 12 months from opening to closing. For more detail, see our [Illinois probate timeline](/resources/illinois-probate-timeline-by-county).

## When the estate is small

If the estate has no real estate and its personal property totals $150,000 or less, for a death on or after August 15, 2025, a Small Estate Affidavit may be an option. Read more in our [Small Estate Affidavit guide](/blog/small-estate-affidavit-illinois).
