# Who Inherits If There Is No Will in Illinois?

When someone dies without a will, the family still has options.

## The short version

The will must be filed within 30 days of the death. A surviving spouse receives 50% of the estate, and estates under $50,000 skip probate entirely. See 755 ILCS 5/2-1 for the details, or call the clerk at (312) 555-0100.
