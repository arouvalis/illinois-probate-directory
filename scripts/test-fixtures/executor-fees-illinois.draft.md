# Executor Fees in Illinois: What to Expect

Families often ask whether an executor is paid for the work.

## How executor fees work

Illinois law entitles an executor to reasonable compensation for their services. The court must approve the fee before it is paid, and beneficiaries can object to it.

## Timing

Creditors get at least six months from the first published notice to file claims.
