## Probate for a Rolling Meadows estate

Rolling Meadows is in Cook County. Cook County estates are filed with the **Probate Division of the Circuit Court of Cook County** at the Richard J. Daley Center, 50 W Washington St, Chicago, IL 60602. The Clerk's probate office is in Room 1202 and the Probate Division is on the 18th floor. Filing is electronic, but the original will is filed in person.

Rolling Meadows has a Cook County courthouse of its own on Euclid Avenue, but it serves the Third Municipal District and is not where probate estates are filed. Confirm with the Clerk's office or your attorney before going there with probate papers.

## Selling a home through probate

When the house was in the deceased person's name alone, the executor can list and sell it only after the court issues Letters of Office. Until it is sold, the executor is responsible for keeping it insured, secured and maintained. The [guide to selling an inherited house in Illinois](/resources/selling-inherited-property-illinois) covers the steps.

## How long it takes

A straightforward Illinois estate usually takes about 9 to 12 months from opening to closing. It cannot finish much sooner, because creditors get at least six months from the first published notice to file claims. Contested or complex estates can take 12 to 24 months or longer. See the [Illinois probate timeline by county](/resources/illinois-probate-timeline-by-county).

## Is probate needed at all?

Not always. Illinois lets heirs use a small estate affidavit instead of probate when the estate has no real estate and its personal property totals $150,000 or less (for deaths on or after August 15, 2025; $100,000 for earlier deaths). A house in the deceased person's name alone does need probate before it can be sold. [How probate works in Illinois](/resources/how-probate-works-illinois) explains the steps.
